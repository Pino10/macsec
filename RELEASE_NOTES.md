## MacSec 0.15.0

### Better AI reviews
Six new review skills: build pipelines (GitHub Actions and similar), smart contracts, sign-in and identity setup, an evidence check every finding must pass, a map to OWASP and other standards, and a guard that tells the AI to treat anything it reads in your project or on a site as information, never as orders.

### What changed since last time
When you run a recon on a domain you scanned before, MacSec now shows new hosts, hosts no longer seen, new endpoints, and new or gone findings.

### DeepSeek and other model servers
Tools now has a Model servers section with presets for DeepSeek and others. The key is kept in the Keychain.
